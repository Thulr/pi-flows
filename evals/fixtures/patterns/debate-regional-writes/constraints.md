# Day-10 constraints

Go live by day 10. Monthly cost must be at most $28k.
Write p99 must be strictly below 175ms; duplicate side effects at most 0.25%.
Failover must be at most 480 seconds; replication lag at most 60 seconds; failover CPU at most 90%.
Use upper-bound measurements and lower-bound control effectiveness. Latency, cost, and percentage-point changes are additive.
Only controls deployable by day 10 count.
