# Publication review architecture

Choose one indivisible option; controls may not be borrowed across options.

- A, single-pass plus sampled review: one model assigns support status. It is the simpler path.
- B, claim ledger plus independent verifier: a second pass checks extracted claims and routes disagreements.

B's approved prelaunch rule checker catches 40% of would-be unsupported publications overall and 30% in the new-supplier cohort. Conservative lower bounds are 35% and 25%. Caught items route to humans and add 6 percentage points to B's review share.

A's only proposed control is a weekly post-publication audit. It discovers errors after release and changes no pre-publication rate.
